Datanous.ai — Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Datanous.ai?
A: Datanous.ai is an AI services company founded in 2021 in Athens, Greece. It builds enterprise RAG systems, hybrid search platforms, and AI governance tools for regulated industries.

Q: What is Datanous Insight?
A: Datanous Insight is a RAG-powered knowledge management platform. It indexes an organisation's internal documents and allows staff to query them through a natural language interface. Every answer includes source citations traceable to the original document.

Q: What document formats does Datanous Insight support?
A: Datanous Insight supports PDF, Microsoft Word (docx), Markdown, HTML, plain text, and CSV files. Integration connectors are available for Notion, Confluence, SharePoint, and Google Drive.

Q: How does Datanous Search differ from a traditional search engine?
A: Traditional search engines rely on keyword matching alone. Datanous Search combines BM25 keyword retrieval with dense semantic embeddings and applies a cross-encoder reranker to the merged candidate set. This hybrid approach retrieves documents that match both the exact words and the meaning of the query, significantly improving recall and precision over keyword-only systems.

Q: What is Datanous Guard and why is it necessary?
A: Datanous Guard is an AI governance layer that enforces factual grounding, detects prompt injection attacks, redacts PII, and prevents cross-tenant data leakage. It is necessary because enterprise AI deployments operate under legal and regulatory obligations. A response that is factually ungrounded, or that exposes one client's data to another, can create serious legal and reputational liability.

Q: What is the uptime SLA for Datanous Insight Enterprise?
A: The Enterprise tier of Datanous Insight carries a 99.9 percent uptime SLA, backed by a dedicated vector store and geographically redundant infrastructure.

Q: What embedding models does Datanous Embed support?
A: Datanous Embed supports text-embedding-3-small (1536 dimensions) and text-embedding-3-large (3072 dimensions). Clients may also bring their own fine-tuned embedding models via the custom model endpoint.

Q: Does Datanous.ai offer on-premises deployment?
A: Yes. Enterprise clients may deploy the full Datanous stack on-premises or in a private cloud. Datanous.ai provides a Kubernetes Helm chart and an implementation guide for self-hosted deployments.

Q: How does Datanous.ai handle data privacy?
A: All data processed by Datanous.ai is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Data is not used to train external models. Clients retain full ownership of their documents and embeddings. Datanous.ai is GDPR-compliant and provides a Data Processing Agreement for all Professional and Enterprise clients.

Q: What is the typical project timeline for a RAG implementation?
A: A standard Datanous Insight deployment for a document corpus of up to 50,000 pages takes four to six weeks. This includes data ingestion, chunking strategy tuning, retrieval quality evaluation, and integration with the client's existing applications via the REST API.
